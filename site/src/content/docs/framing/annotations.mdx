An annotation is a note placed in data coordinates, so it stays on its point at every width. A `text` annotation with a connector points at one mark.

<Demo id="framing/annotations-callout" />

## Text callout

The callout sits at the US point, `x: 75489` and `y: 79.2`. `offset` moves the label 40 pixels left and down from it, `anchor: "bottom"` sets which side of the label faces the point, and `connector: "curve"` draws a curved leader with a ring on the point. `\n` breaks the text into two lines and `**United States**` sets the name in bold.

## Reference line

<Demo id="framing/annotations-refline" />

A `refline` draws a line across the plot at one value. Set `y` for a horizontal line or `x` for a vertical one. This one marks the mean of the twelve monthly highs, 18.2°C, which the demo module computes from the rows, so the line and its label always match the data. `style: "dashed"` keeps it visually separate from the bars.

## Shaded range

<Demo id="framing/annotations-range" />

A `range` shades a stretch of the axis between `x1` and `x2`, or `y1` and `y2`, with an optional `label`. Here it covers the seven quarters when inflation ran above 5%, from the third quarter of 2021 to the first quarter of 2023. `opacity` keeps the fill light enough that the line stays on top, and a y `domain` of 0 to 10 leaves room for the label above the peak.

## Highlighted series

<Demo id="framing/annotations-highlight" />

`highlight: "Coal"` on the color channel keeps coal in its color and greys out the other five series. `seriesStyles` makes the coal line thicker, and one text annotation names the year it first fell below 20%. Keep a chart to three annotations or fewer; one that needs more usually holds two findings and reads better as two charts.

## Fields that matter

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `type` | `"text"`, `"refline"` or `"range"`. |
| `x`, `y` | Data coordinates of a text note or reference line. |
| `x1`, `x2`, `y1`, `y2` | The ends of a range. |
| `text`, `label` | The note text. `**bold**` spans and `\n` line breaks work in `text`. |
| `connector` | `true`, `"curve"`, `"drop-line"` or `{ type, arrow }`. |
| `anchor`, `offset` | Which side of the label faces the point, and the label's pixel offset. |
| `style` | `"solid"`, `"dashed"` or `"dotted"` for a reference line. |
| `encoding.color.highlight` | One or more series to keep in color while the rest turn grey. |
